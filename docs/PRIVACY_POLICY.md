PureEnhance AI processes photos entirely on your device. The app does not request internet access,
does not collect, transmit or share your photos or any personal data, has no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising and no in-app purchases.

* Photos you choose are read through the Android photo picker and processed in memory.
* Enhanced photos are saved only when you tap Save, as a new file in Pictures/PureEnhance. Originals are never modified.
* Saved files keep date/camera metadata from the original but not GPS location.
* Sharing uses the Android share sheet; the app you share to has its own privacy policy.
